## Dancing Mascots 1.0.5

Dancing GIFs on your desktop that move to the tempo of whatever you're listening to.

### Download

- **`Dancing Mascots Setup 1.0.5.exe`** installs it like a normal program (recommended).
- **`Dancing Mascots 1.0.5.exe`** is the portable version: no install, just run it.

Windows 10 or 11. Windows may warn about an "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't signed with a paid
certificate: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### What's new in 1.0.5

- **Restart app button** (and a reminder right after you add a GIF) for the glitch where a mascot you just added
  has a white box around it on some PCs. Restarting the app clears it.
- **The sound reconnects by itself** after a headset reconnect, a wake from sleep, or a changed default output, and
  every so often while nothing is heard. The panel now shows which Windows output device it is listening to.
- **Copy diagnostics** now lists each mascot and whether its window is visible.
- Removed the "Compatibility mode" checkbox from 1.0.1: it didn't help.
- This version goes back to the lighter, faster engine of 1.0.1. Versions 1.0.2 to 1.0.4 were withdrawn because
  they used more memory and CPU.

### Highlights

- Detects the tempo of any sound playing on your PC and speeds the GIFs up or down to match
- Any number of mascots: move, resize, flip and speed each one individually, on any monitor
- Optional bounce on the beat
- Pauses when the music stops, and can pause while a game or video is fullscreen
- Lives in the system tray and can launch with Windows
- Works fully offline: nothing is collected

See the [README](https://github.com/DreamyAkemi/dancingmascots#readme) for a full guide.
